Intel sustainability timeline

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename the newly formed company NM Electronics to Intel Corporation, laying the foundation for decades of technological innovation.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor, igniting the microprocessor revolution and propelling the future of computing devices.

1978

8086 Processor

The 8086 processor establishes the x86 architecture that powers countless PCs and servers today.

1985

386 Processor

Intel introduces the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture, ushering in a new era of performance and multitasking for personal computers.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel's operational greenhouse gas emissions reach a high point. The company then invests in emissions reduction, renewable energy, and efficient manufacturing.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches its RISE strategy and 2030 goals for climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction.

2022

Net-Zero By 2040

Intel commits to achieve net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across its global operations by 2040.

2023

Renewable Electricity

The company achieves 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide, helping lower carbon emissions and advance its long-term sustainability goals.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit, bringing industry, government, and suppliers together to advance sustainable semiconductor manufacturing.

About the goals
